Know Before You Pay

01

No Commission on Sales

100% of every sale is yours. Price your goods however you want. There’s no guest Wi-Fi — make sure your card reader (Square, Venmo, Cash App) works on cellular data.

02

One 6 ft Table or Similar

Plan for one 6 ft table or a similar-size setup — roughly 8 × 5 ft. Exact space varies by venue, so keep your setup compact; we plan aisles and capacity around it.

03

Bring Your Own Setup

We provide the floor space. Bring your own table, chairs, displays, and lighting. Power is available, but battery or rechargeable LED lights give you the most freedom — bring extension cords if you plug in.

04

What You Can Sell

Goods that need a table: jewelry, apparel, candles, accessories, sculpture, and prints under 8×10 in. Wall art goes on the walls — apply as an artist for that.

Not allowed: beverages, cannabis or smoking products, food cooked on-site, weapons, or non-art products and advertising.
05

Load-In & Placement

Load-in is listed in your portal. Don’t arrive early — we won’t be ready for you. Spaces are first come, first served, so be on time.

06

Show Night

Your space includes 1 entry; anyone else staying for the show needs a General Admission ticket. Load-out starts after 11 PM, and everything leaves that night — the venue disposes of anything left behind.
